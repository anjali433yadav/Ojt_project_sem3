
CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. PHISHING EMAILS

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers use fake emails or messages
to trick people into sharing confidential information or performing
unsafe actions.

Warning signs include:
- Unknown or suspicious sender addresses.
- Unexpected attachments.
- Urgent messages demanding immediate action.
- Requests for passwords or confidential information.
- Links that do not match the claimed website.

Employees should verify suspicious messages through official channels
and report them to their security team.

2. PASSWORD SECURITY

Employees should:
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Never share passwords with anyone.
- Enable multi-factor authentication.
- Avoid reusing passwords across different services.
- Never enter passwords through suspicious links.

3. SAFE BROWSING

Employees should:
- Verify website addresses before entering information.
- Avoid downloading files from untrusted websites.
- Keep browsers and operating systems updated.
- Avoid clicking unexpected links.
- Use approved company resources.

4. REPORTING SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY

Employees should:
- Report suspicious emails to the security team.
- Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
- Never enter real credentials into simulation pages.
- Follow the organization's incident reporting process.

5. PHISHING AWARENESS SIMULATION

A phishing simulation is a controlled cybersecurity training
exercise designed to teach employees how to identify suspicious
messages.

All simulation messages must be clearly controlled training
content. They must not collect real passwords or confidential data.
